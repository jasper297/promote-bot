## WHAT IS PROMOTE.FUN

promote.fun is a performance clipping platform. Brands pay for campaigns. Creators ("clippers") redistribute brand content as short-form clips on TikTok, Instagram, YouTube. Creators earn based on views. Brands get viral reach at low CPM.

Website: https://promote.fun
Minimum client budget: $2,000
Platform fee: deducted from campaign total (e.g. $5K campaign = $1K fee + $4K to creators)
Payout models: CPM (per 1K views), Fixed (per post), Hybrid
Creator payouts: Solana USDC via Vault module. Status: pending → available after approval.
